# Scientific summary

The campaign fitted 41 distinct VNA triplets. Across 1,784 acquisitions
and 5,352 run-mode rows, 324 rows support conditional cavity
characterizations, 276 remain ambiguous and 4,752 are unassigned.
The archive contains 27 multi-run intervals with matching recorded
configuration. TM012 has conditional spans 1148–1219, 1426–1428 and
1429–1669. The TM012 fits associated with Runs 1426 and 1429 differ by
+0.780 MHz in fitted resonance and +18.5% in loaded Q.

All 17 TM010 fits carry a large-residual flag, and two are sensitive to
starting values. None of the 41 triplets has an accurate covariance
determination for the selected offline fit. Formal parameter errors
and differences are therefore descriptive. Verified trace identity and
recorded configuration continuity do not establish operational selection
or physical applicability. The Run 1429 comparison is confined to the
shared RF band and retains ADC-plane PSD units of V²/Hz.

The next scientific use is a cavity-shape comparison within supported
TM012 spans, with explicit frequency registration and uncertainty labels.
Sweep routing, mode-qualified selection and sideband evidence remain
open. The presentation makes no RF-to-ADC normalization or absolute
noise-temperature claim.

The revised appendix contains all 41 fitted triplets and their original
residual panels. The authoritative FLASH-logbook snapshot contributes
1,814 dated acquisitions. Temporal candidate intervals are reported
under both VNA clock hypotheses and remain separate from the
evidence-supported characterization spans. Run 680 remains a historical
reference in the tables and appendix.
