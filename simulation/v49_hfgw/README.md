# Conditional QUAX GHz strain and PBH benchmarks

The analysis uses the archived run-680 common-mask science PSD from Step 10C. H and N are independent alternatives for the **coherent direct** RF/readout gain. The same observed noise is used for both. No noise, calibration or run data are refit.

## Apparatus and reference scale

Spasiano, Chapter 3, reports an OFHC cavity of inner radius 13.51 mm and height 246 mm. The nominal empty cylinder is 0.141 L and the copper rod reduces it to 0.139 L (pp. 49, 51). Chapter 3 reports 8.5 T central and 6.73 T RMS in the cavity (p. 47). Chapter 7 uses 6.73 T and rounded 0.14 L. A read-only inspection of the run-680 beginning-of-run ODB found run metadata and no scalar magnet field/current state among keys containing magnet, field-specific identifiers, tesla, persistent, solenoid or current. The field map/current timeline for this run remains unverified. The calculation is conditional on the thesis field. This is QUAX GHz. The larger FLASH MHz volume and field in GravNet are separate apparatus. The Run2 cavity wall sensor proxy is 40.42 mK for the reference interval. The run calibration setup is documented without TWPA in Chapter 6 and Step 9A. The wall proxy and 3.955 K equivalent receiver temperature describe different quantities.

The mode frequencies, Q0 and coupling parameters are the approved Step 6 / thesis Table 6.1 inputs. QL = Q0/(1+κ1+κ2). For intrinsic-loss normalized κ1, κ2, the readout branching fraction is κ2/(1+κ1+κ2), the two-port generalization of β/(1+β) in GravNet Eq. 7 and thesis Eq. 7.9. The stationary extracted power is K h² at resonance, with K = (κ2/(1+κ1+κ2)) (2π f0)/(2 μ0) B_RMS² QL C_GW V. B²/μ0 is energy density, ω supplies inverse time, so K has units W per unit strain squared. At detuning ν, multiply by |L(ν)|², where L = 1/(1+2iν/b) and b=f0/QL is the full half-power bandwidth.

Thesis Eq. 7.10 is the normalized squared overlap of the mode electric field and the TT effective current for a specified direction and polarization, with the field-map and mode normalizations in the denominator. Fig. 7.1 has six directional plots. Numerical maps, calibrated orientation/time mapping and relative polarization phases were not recovered. Axion overlap values are not used. The **reference C_GW = 0.01 is illustrative**, applied to one specified effective polarization independently in each mode. For other C_GW, B or volume, multiply strain thresholds by (6.73 T/B) sqrt[(0.01/C_GW)(0.139 L/V)]. Signals and distance reach scale inversely to this. At a coupling null the inferred sensitivity diverges. Face-on circular polarization is represented by the peak amplitude of one component only. Coherent two-polarization response would need the complex overlaps. [GravNet Eqs. 5–9](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.24645v1#S2.SS3)

## Strain conversion and normalization

Define A(f) = sqrt[50 Ω × 10^(G_RF/10) × K] |H_direct(f)|, with G_RF from Step 9A L5 + nominal 36 dB. H_direct is 0.01 kz for H or the nominal ideal complex main response for N. It is evaluated at the physical science IF. The calibration tone plane, FET uncertainty, nominal loading and frequencies retain Step 9A–9E limitations. The equivalent port noise is S_z/(50 Ω × G_RF,linear × |H_direct|²). Equivalent peak-strain complex ASD is sqrt(S_z)/|A L|. S_z is bilateral in signed complex ADC coordinates and integrates to E|I−iQhardware|². An equivalent real narrowband one-sided strain ASD, in the convention that the complex envelope amplitude equals the physical sinusoid's peak, is the quoted complex ASD / sqrt(2). Neither definition adds a folded noise gain to the coherent signal.

The science PSD was measured after the common projection. The direct benchmark uses the calibrated positive-IF branch, within its first Nyquist support. RF image noise and ADC aliases already contribute to S_z. Signal outside this positive direct template support, signal aliases and conjugate leakage are not assigned template power. Their phase and amplitude are incompletely determined. For TM010 the template band covers about 92% of the ideal Lorentzian **port** energy, which is not a bound on omitted ADC SNR. The mean measured PSD is averaged in blocks of 128 adjacent bins then linearly evaluated only between supported centers. This estimates the local noise density without interpolation across DC. The center statistical PSD SE from 50 groups is about 0.12–0.15% and is not a full uncertainty on strain. The tables include fitted-VNA statistical errors. Unmeasured field, directional overlaps, delivered calibration tone, FET and loaded electronics are systematic and unbounded here. For a known gain shift ΔG dB, h scales as 10^(−ΔG/20).

The ideal detector statistic is a fixed-phase matched amplitude with Gaussian proper noise approximation, ρ² = 2∫|s̃|²/S_z df. SNR in amplitude is ρ, its squared noncentrality is ρ². On-resonance coherent rectangular observation uses h_ρ = ρ ASD/sqrt(2T), ENBW 1/T. The 209.7152 s row assumes uninterrupted coherence across the equivalent acquired duration, without claiming that the run has that continuity. The transient table evaluates an incident rectangular strain envelope of duration T using its T sinc(νT) transform, cavity response and supported noise weighting. Thresholds are illustrative SNR values, without trials factor, false alarm, efficiency or a detection/exclusion interpretation. Improper science noise and nonstationarity would require validating the statistic and possibly using a full covariance matched filter.

## Inspiral models and checks

The grid covers individual equal masses 10⁻¹⁵–10⁻⁷ solar masses. M_total=2m and chirp mass=2^(−1/5)m. Circular leading-order quadrupole GR in SI gives d f/dt = (96/5)π^(8/3)(G Mchirp/c³)^(5/3) f^(11/3) and face-on single-polarization peak h = 4(G Mchirp/c³)^(5/3)(π f)^(2/3)c/D. These reproduce the scalings of GravNet Eqs. 5 and 8. The Eq. 5 prefactor agrees with individual mass in the numerical normalization (about 460 versus 462 GHz/s at m=10⁻⁹ solar masses and f=1 GHz), whereas interpreting its mass as total gives 145 GHz/s. Eq. 8's printed 9.77e−34 compares with 9.43e−34 for individual m=10⁻¹² solar masses at 1 kpc and 1 GHz in SI. We use the explicit SI convention. Eq. 9 is characteristic strain, with extra chirp-cycle weighting, and is not the driving amplitude. Equation 1 is approximate and uses angular frequency. We independently check Schwarzschild f_ISCO = c³/(6^(3/2)π G M_total) as the inspiral cutoff sanity check. The grid stays below it near the modes. Near the upper mass edge, post-Newtonian, spin and merger effects are not validated. No rate or source-population assumption enters.

Locally freeze h and the chirp rate α at passage. With γ=πb, solve the causal complex envelope equation dy/dt+γy=γ exp(iπαt²), normalized so the peak steady on-resonance output is sqrt(K) h sqrt(W). The analytic convolution uses erfcx, and the local chirp Fourier transform is exp(iπ/4−iπν²/α)/sqrt(α). The cavity Lorentzian appears once in either representation. The full local-linear ideal port energy is K h² πb/(2α), from the Lorentzian area, compared to the rectangular estimate in GravNet Eq. 6. It remains valid for a rapid passage without a steady-state ring-up assumption. The drive is a tangent approximation near passage and should not be interpreted as a physical inspiral far from resonance. The plotted incident frequency/strain interval is restricted to ±3 linewidths. At fixed RF, h grows as m^(5/3), crossing time falls as m^(−5/3), and this local ideal matched SNR grows as m^(5/6). Reach for SNR 1 and 5 scales as 1/D and uses the supported band.

For intermediate and fast ADC waveforms the analytic chirp spectrum and causal cavity pole are projected through the supported direct IF with nominal or measured magnitude and zero additional readout phase. The time series is then synthesized at 5 MS/s, downstream of this band selection. The slow figure uses the exact cavity envelope and an adiabatic coherent readout snapshot, with the SNR still integrated from the supported Fresnel spectrum over the entire passage. Unknown electronic phases affect time-domain templates and observability. Output NPZs store time, I/Q convention, amplitude, phase, cavity state, incident frequency and strain, and experimental PSD and noise-overlay arrays separately. ADC benchmark voltages are linear input-referred voltages at the ADC connector before any extra digitization. The overlays reuse representative **simulated** quantized Step 10A noise. They are not injections into raw science records or independent realizations across benchmark cases. The original physical signals are shown at 1 AU. A separate plot shows illustrative SNR-5 injections, with exact peak strain and equivalent distance in the benchmark table. A known template may reach SNR 5 without being visibly distinguishable in a single ADC snapshot.

Validation checks include half-power normalization, Lorentzian area, numerical time-domain energy agreement including cavity ring-down, exact versus quadrature intermediate solution, slow and impulse limits, ISCO, direct-template Fourier Parseval, and noise-weighted integral convergence under grid coarsening. The validation JSON contains measured numerical residuals. BENCHMARK and reach results are conditional sensitivities, without an astrophysical exclusion or validated detection threshold.
